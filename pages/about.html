About

Ziwei Lin ("zee-way") is a documentary filmmaker and video-journalist based in Williamstown, Massachusetts. She is a senior at Williams College studying Comparative Literature, and a board of directors member of Purple Valley Productions, William's student-run filmmaking club.

Her work finds its subject in the margins: ethnic minorities navigating cultural erasure in southwest China, communities fighting environmental racism in rural Florida, traditions balanced on the edge of disappearance. She has conducted over 60 hours of interviews across 15 villages, and her films have screened at the Independent Short Fest Los Angeles and Discovery, among others.

Ziwei grew up in Beijing, China. While not working, she likes to catch screenings at Metrograph and hang out with her cat.
